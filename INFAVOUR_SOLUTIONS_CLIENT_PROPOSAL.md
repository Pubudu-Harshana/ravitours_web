## 3. Scope of Work & Deliverables

### Phase 1: Core Portal Architecture & High-Prestige Branding
- **Custom Modern Frontend:** Engineered using Next.js App Router, React, and Tailwind CSS.
- **Brand Identity & Color Psychology:** Curated palette featuring Deep Oceanic Blue (`#07345A`), Ceylon Gold (`#F6A623`), and Coastal Cyan (`#087EA4`).
- **Responsive PWA Architecture:** Zero app-store barrier—loads identically across iPhones, Android smartphones, tablets, and widescreen laptops.

### Phase 2: High-Yield Customer Acquisition Engines
- **Interactive Trip Planner:** Dynamic 3-step tool where tourists choose destination preferences, duration, and interests, generating instant WhatsApp/email bookings.
- **Tour Showcase & Day-by-Day Itineraries:** Structured itineraries detailing daily highlights, vehicle inclusions, and transparent pricing.
- **Destination Explorer:** Comprehensive travel hubs for Sri Lanka’s top regions (Sigiriya, Ella, Kandy, Nuwara Eliya, Yala, Mirissa).
- **Service Diversification:** Fleet showcase (sedans, KDH vans, SUVs), 24/7 CMB airport transfers, safari jeep reservations, and boutique hotel bookings.

### Phase 3: Conversion Optimization & Social Proof Engine
- **Persistent WhatsApp & Direct Call Widgets:** Instant communication trigger on every page.
- **Trust & Credibility Hub:** Verified foreign traveler testimonials, safety guarantees, driver licensing badges, and cancellation policies.
- **Special Promotional Campaigns:** Discount countdown tags and seasonal deal cards to stimulate off-season sales.

### Phase 4: Technical SEO & Cloud Infrastructure
- **Search Engine Optimization:** Built-in `sitemap.ts`, `robots.ts`, structured metadata, and OpenGraph social preview tags.
- **Global Edge Deployment:** Deployed on enterprise cloud networks (Vercel / Cloudflare) ensuring sub-second loading worldwide.
