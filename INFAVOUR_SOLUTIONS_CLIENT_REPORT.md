## 🚀 Detailed System Capabilities: What You Can Do With This Website

### 1. 🧭 Interactive "Plan Your Trip" Custom Builder
* **Location:** Accessible across the homepage and dedicated `/plan-your-trip` route.
* **How It Works:**
  - Visitors select their dream destination (Cultural Triangle, Hill Country, Wildlife Safari, Southern Beaches, etc.).
  - They choose their travel duration (e.g., 3-5 days, 6-8 days, 9-12 days, 14+ days).
  - They click multi-select travel interests (Beaches, Culture, Wildlife, Adventure, Romance/Honeymoon).
  - With a single click, an inquiry is generated and routed either to your database/email or sent **directly to your WhatsApp** with an automated, pre-formatted message:
    > *"Hello Ravi Tours & Travels! I am planning a Sri Lanka trip. Destination preference: Ella & Hill Country | Duration: 6-8 days | Interests: Wildlife, Culture | Name: John Doe"*
* **Why It’s Worth High Value:** You bypass cold forms that get ignored. You receive ready-to-close leads directly on WhatsApp while the customer is hot.

### 2. 🧳 Comprehensive Tour Showcase & Itinerary Engine
* **Location:** `/tours` and individual tour pages (e.g., `/tours/classic-sri-lanka-discovery`).
* **Capabilities:**
  - Categorized tours: **Cultural & Heritage, Wildlife & Nature, Beach & Relaxation, Adventure & Hill Country, Luxury Honeymoons, and Quick Excursions**.
  - **Day-by-Day Visual Itineraries:** Each day clearly details morning activities, afternoon highlights, transport, and night stay.
  - **Inclusions & Exclusions Breakdown:** Clear items (air-conditioned vehicle, fuel, highway tolls, chauffeur guide, breakfast, airport transfers) that eliminate buyer confusion.
  - **Direct Inquiry Modals:** Every tour has a dedicated "Inquire About This Tour" action with auto-populated tour titles.
* **Why It’s Worth High Value:** Customers looking to spend $1,000–$5,000 need thorough clarity. This layout looks like a world-class travel agency (comparable to Kuoni or Intrepid Travel), making foreign clients feel 100% safe booking with you.
