1. Project Context & Commercial Opportunity

The international travel market to Sri Lanka is seeing historic surges in high-net-worth travelers from the United Kingdom, Europe, Australia, North America, and the Gulf. However, independent tour operators face two severe commercial barriers:

  • Crushing Middleman Fees: Online Travel Agencies (Viator, GetYourGuide, TripAdvisor, Booking.com) demand 20% to 30% commission on every excursion booked, eroding operating profit margins.
  • Timezone & Trust Hesitation: International guests looking to spend $1,500 – $4,000 require immediate booking reassurance and visual transparency before paying advance deposits.

3. Detailed Scope of Work & Deliverables

Module A: Custom Web Portal & Modern Design System

Custom-built application utilizing Next.js App Router, React, and Tailwind CSS. Modern luxury branding palette featuring Deep Oceanic Blue (#07345A), Ceylon Gold (#F6A623), and Coastal Cyan (#087EA4). Mobile-first, responsive, and ultra-fast.

Module B: Interactive Trip Planner & WhatsApp Engine

Bespoke 3-step holiday builder allowing guests to customize travel destinations, duration, and preferences. Integrated direct WhatsApp lead generator with automated itinerary pre-formatting for instant mobile quotation.

Module C: Tour Catalog & Day-by-Day Itineraries

Multi-tier tour showcase (Cultural, Wildlife, Beaches, Hill Country, Honeymoons) featuring day-by-day morning/evening agendas, vehicle fleet details, transparent Inclusions/Exclusions, and direct inquiry modals.

Module D: Sri Lanka Destination Knowledge Hub

Comprehensive destination guides (Sigiriya, Ella, Kandy, Nuwara Eliya, Yala, Mirissa, Galle) engineered for organic Google SEO discovery and cross-tour linking.

Module E: Transport & Fleet Monetization Showcase

Service showcase covering private chauffeur-guides (sedans, luxury KDH vans, SUVs), 24/7 CMB Airport transfers with flight monitoring, safari jeeps, and scenic train ticket reservations.

Module F: International Social Proof & Trust Hub

Verified international traveler testimonials, government registration badges, 24/7 on-tour safety guarantees, and clear booking transparency.
