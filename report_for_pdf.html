3. Detailed System Capabilities: What You Can Do

A. Interactive Custom Trip Builder (/plan-your-trip)

Allows foreign guests to design their dream holiday across 3 steps: selecting destinations (Cultural Triangle, Hill Country, Wildlife, Beaches), durations (3-5 days up to 14+ days), and travel preferences. Generates an automated, pre-formatted WhatsApp & email notification containing full passenger details so you can immediately respond with a custom quote.

B. Multi-Day Tour Itinerary Showcase (/tours)

Presents detailed, day-by-day travel itineraries (Cultural Heritage, Wildlife Expeditions, Coastal Escapes, Luxury Honeymoons). Each tour page provides morning and evening schedules, high-resolution photography, vehicle details, transparent Inclusions/Exclusions, and direct inquiry modals.

C. Sri Lanka Destination Knowledge Hub (/destinations)

Features comprehensive destination guides for Sigiriya, Ella, Kandy, Nuwara Eliya, Yala National Park, Mirissa, and Galle. Provides travelers with photography insights, best visiting months, weather tips, and links to matching tour itineraries.

D. Multi-Service Monetization Portfolio (/services)

Enables cross-selling beyond package tours into multiple high-margin revenue streams:

  • Private Chauffeur & Vehicle Fleet: Air-conditioned sedans, luxury KDH vans, and SUVs with licensed English-speaking driver guides.
  • 24/7 Airport Meet-and-Greet Transfers (CMB): Punctual pickups with real-time flight tracking and arrivals hall greeting.
  • Hotel & Resort Booking Support: Preferred partner rates across luxury heritage bungalows and beach resorts.
  • Excursions & Wildlife Safaris: Yala/Udawalawe jeep bookings, Mirissa whale watching, and scenic Kandy-to-Ella train ticketing.

E. Trust Engine & Verified Traveler Social Proof

Eliminates international buyer hesitation through real guest reviews from the UK, Australia, Germany, and the USA, reinforced with safety badges, 24/7 on-tour emergency support, and full passenger insurance guarantees.
